# Try the preview effects

Open the preview with Ctrl+Shift+V. The **Effects** button at its top right
has a switch for everything below.

## Typing

Put the caret at the end of the word load and type `ing`. The new letters
start warm and settle into the text color. The spotlight marks the word.

Type a second word after it. Each word settles on its own clock.

## Paste and delete

Copy a few words, then paste them into this sentence. The preview washes and
underlines them, and the source boxes them.

Select three or four words in this sentence and press Delete. The words float
above the place they left, struck through, and the line below does not move.

## Selection and caret

Select from here to **this bold text** in the source. The same text lights up
in the preview. Clear the selection and the highlight goes with it.

Turn on **Follow the source caret**, then press Ctrl+End. The preview goes
with you. Turn it off and scroll the preview by hand: it stays where you put it.

## Peek

Rest the pointer on [this link to the Tasks section](#tasks), then on
[this outside link](https://commonmark.org/help/). Hold Ctrl and move the
pointer over the list below to see its Markdown.

## Far away

The paragraphs below only make the page long enough to scroll.

One. Linework keeps the preview where you are reading.

Two. An edit above your reading position does not push the text you are on.

Three. Show edit appears at the edge when an edit lands out of view.

Four. With Follow on, the preview comes to the caret instead.

Five. Reduce motion keeps the colors and drops the movement.

Six. Every effect here can be switched off on its own.
